# Desi Creator Outreach Playbook

Indian indie creators (musicians, bedroom producers, illustrators, visual designers) receive two types of messages:
1. **Generic corporate recruiter spam:** *"Dear creator, we are a leading platform seeking synergies..."* (Ignored 99% of the time).
2. **Unpaid "exposure" beggars:** *"Bro can you make an album cover for free, we give shoutout."* (Blocked immediately).

To achieve an **80%+ response rate**, the outreach must follow the **3 Golden Laws of Indie Outreach**:
1. **Specific Proof of Work:** Name a specific song, track timestamp, Behance project, or illustration detail you genuinely observed.
2. **Transparent Commercial Intent:** State clearly that this is a paid collaboration / contract gig with budget allocated.
3. **Respect for Creative Autonomy:** Give them creative room rather than micro-managing.

---

## Template 1: Casual Instagram DM (Indie Singer / Music Producer)
*Use for artists discovered on Bandcamp / Spotify / SoundCloud with active IG bios.*

```text
Hey [Name]! Loved [Specific Track Name] - especially the [mention specific element, e.g. "dreamy synth progression in the outro" / "vocal texture in the second verse"]. So refreshing to hear that sound coming out of [City/India].

I’m [Your Name], building [App/Startup Name] (we’re building [1-sentence plain English product description]). 

We’re putting together our launch [video soundtrack / in-app audio cues / promotional campaign] and wanted to see if you’d be open to a paid collaboration or licensing a track. We have budget set aside and want to keep your authentic sound front and center.

If you’re open to exploring this, let me know where’s best to send a quick 1-pager or voice note (email or WhatsApp). No pressure either way, keep making great music!
```

## Template 2: Direct Email (Indie Visual Designer / Brand Illustrator)
*Use for Behance / Bento / Personal site portfolios with public email addresses.*

**Subject:** Collaboration: [Startup Name] × [Artist Name] (Paid Design Project)

```text
Hi [Name],

Came across your portfolio on [Behance/Bento/ReadCV] and was blown away by [Specific Project Name] - the [mention specific visual trait, e.g. "texture work and typography balance" / "how you handled the character identity without making it look like generic SaaS slop"].

I’m [Your Name], founder/operator at [Startup Name]. We’re building [1-line mission, e.g. "a modern financial terminal for young Indian operators"].

We’re currently redesigning our [core brand identity / custom illustration library / launch landing page] and are actively looking for an independent designer with your specific aesthetic to lead the visuals. 

Details:
• Scope: [e.g. 5 custom hero illustrations / 1 brand identity kit / 1 micro-site design]
• Timeline: Flexible around [e.g. Next 3-4 weeks]
• Commercials: Fully paid project (happy to work with your standard project or day rates)
• Freedom: High autonomy - we want your signature voice, not a corporate committee brief.

Would love to hop on a quick 15-min chat this week or share the full brief over email if you have bandwidth. 

Warmly,
[Your Name]
[Twitter/LinkedIn or App Link]
[Phone/WhatsApp if desired]
```

## Template 3: The "Founding Team / Core Hire" Invitation
*Use when scouting for a Founding Designer, Creative Director, or Audio Lead for an early-stage startup.*

**Subject:** Building [Startup Name] - Loved your work on [Project Name]

```text
Hey [Name],

I've been following your work on [Behance / ReadCV / Twitter], particularly your breakdown of [Project Name]. The craft and intentionality in your work stands out completely from the sea of Figma templates and AI-generated noise.

I'm [Your Name], founder of [Startup Name] ([backed by X / building Y]). We’re assembling our core founding team and looking for a Founding Designer / Creative Lead who has high aesthetic taste and deep craft.

We care deeply about craftsmanship - our product has to look and feel like art, not just enterprise software.

I don’t know if you’re currently exploring full-time or fractional roles, but I’d love to buy you a coffee (or jump on a quick Google Meet) just to share what we're building and learn more about what you want to create next.

Are you free for 20 mins sometime this Thursday or Friday?

Best,
[Your Name]
```
