# 5-Vector Creative Evaluation Rubric (Max Score: 50)

Every scouted candidate (singer, sound designer, illustrator, brand identity craftsman, UI artist) is graded across 5 distinct vectors from 0 to 10.

---

## 1. Aesthetic & Taste Alignment (`score_aesthetic_fit`: 0-10)
Measures the depth, sophistication, and direct resonance of their craft with the user's target vibe.
* **9-10 (Exceptional):** The work is breathtakingly aligned with the desired vibe; exhibits rare taste, bespoke typography/soundscapes, and zero generic tropes.
* **7-8 (Strong):** Clear stylistic coherence; minor adjustments needed to fit the exact application.
* **4-6 (Moderate):** Competent execution, but relies on familiar or slightly dated templates/motifs.
* **0-3 (Weak):** Generic slop, stock imagery, derivative soundpacks, or stylistic mismatch.

---

## 2. Underground Authenticity & Craft Depth (`score_authenticity`: 0-10)
Evaluates whether the creator has genuine artistic conviction vs algorithmic trend-chasing.
* **9-10 (Hidden Gem):** Cult following or completely uncommoditized (<10k followers/listeners); creates out of raw obsession; distinctive personal signature.
* **7-8 (Emerging Voice):** Modest organic traction (10k-30k); loyal niche audience; clear creative voice.
* **4-6 (Commercial / Diluted):** 30k-75k followers; portfolio shows signs of chasing viral reels or agency boilerplate.
* **0-3 (Over-leveraged / Mass-Market):** Macro-influencer, celebrity rate card, ghost-produced, or lacks personal craft.

---

## 3. Collaboration Realism & Commercial Viability (`score_collab_realism`: 0-10)
Determines whether this creator can realistically work with a startup, brand, or early-stage team.
* **9-10 (High Realism):** Fully independent; manages own inquiries; explicitly open to freelance commissions, sync licensing, or contract projects; reasonable budget expectations.
* **7-8 (Good Realism):** Independent; busy but reachable; requires clear project scoping.
* **4-6 (Complex):** Has part-time management or informal agency representation; slower turnaround.
* **0-3 (Unrealistic):** Exclusive label/agency lock-in, unreachable pricing (corporate tier), or unresponsive.

---

## 4. Contact Reachability & Direct Channel (`score_reachability`: 0-10)
How quickly and directly can the user or founder open a conversation?
* **9-10 (Direct Direct):** Active personal email in bio (`mailto:`), direct WhatsApp Business link in Linktree, or open Instagram DMs with high response rate.
* **7-8 (Verified Path):** Active Behance messaging, Substack/ReadCV direct form, or monitored Twitter/X DM.
* **4-6 (Indirect):** Generic agency/label inquiry email (`info@agency.com`), contact form only.
* **0-3 (Dead End):** No public contact paths or dormant social profiles (>1 year inactive).

---

## 5. Marketing / Product Value Multiplier (`score_marketing_multiplier`: 0-10)
Measures how much this creator's work or aesthetic will elevate the user's startup or product.
* **9-10 (Viral / Brand Defining):** A collaboration would be an instant PR/credibility flex; users will ask *"who did this artwork/song?"*; elevates product perceived value 10x.
* **7-8 (Substantial Lift):** Strong aesthetic polish; makes landing pages or launch videos stand out markedly from generic SaaS.
* **4-6 (Standard Asset):** Good functional asset (e.g. clean icon set, decent background beat), but doesn't create emotional resonance.
* **0-3 (Negligible):** Forgettable asset that adds little to the user's brand equity.

---

## Priority Bands
* **High Priority (`High`):** Total Score $\ge$ **38/50** (and no vector $< 6$). Move immediately to outreach.
* **Medium Priority (`Medium`):** Total Score **28-37/50**. Good backup or alternative lane.
* **Low Priority (`Low`):** Total Score **$< 28/50$**. Reject or archive.
